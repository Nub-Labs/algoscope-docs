# AlgoScope Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

AlgoScope is a study app for data structures and algorithms. This page explains what the app
does with data, in plain language.

**The short version: nothing leaves your device.** There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and
the app makes no network requests of its own. Everything works offline because everything is on
your device.

## What the app stores on your device

- Lesson content, bundled with the app when you install it.
- Your app settings, such as whether predict mode is on.
- Nothing else. Appearance and reduced motion follow your system settings. Values you type into a
  lesson, such as your own input array or a search term, are used on screen and are not saved
  when you leave the lesson. No progress is stored, so uninstalling the app removes everything.

## What the app sends

Nothing.

The published versions of AlgoScope contain no analytics service, no telemetry, no crash
reporting and no advertising identifier. No usage data is recorded, queued or transmitted, and
there is no server for it to go to.

**What is never collected**: your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, anything you
type, or an advertising identifier. There are no accounts, so nothing is linked to a person.

## Links that leave the app

Each lesson has a **Read more** section with links to outside material, such as Wikipedia or
cp-algorithms. Tapping one opens your browser. The app never loads those pages inside itself, and
we are not told which links you open. Once your browser is open, that site's own privacy policy
applies.

## Problem reports you choose to send

**Report a problem**, in Settings and in every lesson's menu, composes a message about a typo, a
wrong step or a broken picture. The app does not send it. It opens the message in your own mail
app addressed to the contact address below, or as a new issue on our public GitHub page, or
copies it to your clipboard, and nothing leaves your device until you send it yourself.

The message contains what you see on screen before sending: the lesson, example and step, the
problem category, any details you typed, and the app and operating system version. Sending it by
email shows us your email address, as any email does; we use it only to reply about that report.
A report on the public GitHub page is visible to everyone, under your GitHub account.

## Who receives the data

Nobody. There is no server, no third-party service and no data processor, because there is no
data to send. We do not sell data, do not share it, and do not use anything for advertising.

## Children

AlgoScope is intended for students aged 13 and over. The app collects nothing from anyone, of any
age.

## Security

The app holds no account, password or personal data, so there is nothing on our side that could
expose you if it were breached.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the
processing of personal data. We hold no personal data about you at all, so there is nothing to
access or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. For anything
else, contact us at the address below and we will help within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app starts collecting anything, for example usage analytics or crash
reports, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date, and the
app will show a setting to control it. The current version is always at this address.
